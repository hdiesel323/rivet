RIVET
MVP · 2026 · github.com/hdiesel323/rivet

One API. Your providers. Zero toll.

Rivet is the token plane in front of models you already pay for. Point any OpenAI SDK at it. It routes, caches, falls back, and writes a ledger row. It does not resell tokens.

Pricing rule

SurfacePrice
Connectors + orchestration$0
Tokens on their vendor$0 from Rivet
Hosted Rivet compute / agentsbilled later

Not OpenRouter

OpenRouter is a merchant of record. Rivet is a switchboard on the customer’s own keys or local runtime. No catalog tax.

Shape

app → Rivet /v1/chat/completions
        ├ classifier slot
        ├ cache
        ├ router (cheap-first)
        ├ OpenAI-compatible worker
        └ ledger receipt

Receipt fields

model_used · why · vendor_usd · rivet_usd=0 · usage
