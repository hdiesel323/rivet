RIVET
Proof of idea · Sept 2026 · github.com/hdiesel323/rivet

One API. Your providers. Zero toll.

Rivet is the control plane in front of models you already pay for. OAuth-connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Azure, or a local runtime. Rivet routes, falls back, and prints a receipt. It does not resell tokens.

Pricing rule

SurfacePrice
Connectors + orchestration$0
Tokens on their vendor$0 from Rivet
Hosted Rivet compute / agentsbilled later

Not OpenRouter

OpenRouter is a merchant of record. Rivet is a switchboard on the customer’s own grants. No catalog tax.

Shape

app → Rivet /v1/chat/completions
        ├ cache
        ├ router (cheap-first)
        ├ OAuth providers
        └ local adapter

Receipt fields

model_used · why · vendor_usd · rivet_usd=0 · escalated
